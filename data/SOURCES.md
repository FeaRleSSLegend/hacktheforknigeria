# SOURCES

One entry per source: what it is, which rows use it, quality, caveats.

## Carried over (in data/raw/)
- wafct_protein_verified.csv: FAO/INFOODS West African Food Composition Table 2012 (fao.org/3/i2698b/i2698b.pdf). Protein, water, fat. Boiled values are calculated from raw, not measured.
- nbs_prices_history.csv: NBS Selected Food Price Watch national averages (brown beans, boneless beef, frozen chicken). Rows with status press_quote_verify come from news reports and need checking against the NBS tables.
- units_mudu.csv: 1 mudu of beans or grains is about 1.5 kg (trade convention, estimated until someone weighs it).

## Added Nov 20 (data lane)
All paper rows were transcribed from open-access text (core.ac.uk, AJOL, publisher pages) by text extraction and are NOT yet checked against the PDF. Every row's method_note says so. A human must spot-check at least one row per paper before the demo. Page fields hold the table number where the page was not legible; fill in the page when you open the PDF.

### Composition (data/ingredients.csv)
- FAO/INFOODS West African Food Composition Table 2019 (WAFCT_2019.xlsx, sheet "03 NV_sum_39 (per 100g EP)"), dry raw values per 100 g. Values in square brackets in the source are imputed or borrowed, so rows with them are flagged `estimated` and the notes name the fields. It replaces the 2012 table in data/raw (e.g. cowpea protein 21.2 in 2012 vs 22.6 in 2019).
- Cowpea price: NBS Selected Food Price Watch, Feb 2025, 'Beans Brown' national average 2465.96 NGN/kg (white beans 2114.33). Other ingredients have no price yet.

### Functional properties of untreated flour (data/functional_properties.csv), 56 values
- arawande2010: Arawande & Borokini 2010, J. Emerging Trends Eng. Appl. Sci. 1(1):89-95. Jack bean, pigeon pea, cowpea, Table 3. Text and table disagree on one cowpea OAC value (113.50 vs 113.15); the table is used.
- oloyo2005: Oloyo & Akoja 2005, Tropical Agricultural Research and Extension, pigeon pea cv. IITA 8860. Volume and page range not confirmed (extracted text was garbled).
- okoye2021: Okoye-Okeke et al. 2021, Indonesian Food Sci. Technol. J. 5(1):26-33. Only the unsieved pigeon pea flour (P110) is used; sieved variants are particle-size experiments.
- adediran2013: Adediran et al. 2013, Croat. J. Food Technol. Biotechnol. Nutr. 8(3-4):102-110. Only the 100 % cowpea row.
- abulude2006: Abulude et al. 2006, Pak. J. Sci. Ind. Res. 49(6):427-430. Raw melon (Cucumeropsis edulis).
- nzeagwu2018: Nzeagwu & Raphael 2018, J. Dietitians Assoc. Nigeria 9:36-46. Sample 404 (100 % melon seed flour).
- onwuzurike2021: Onwuzurike et al. 2021, Niger. J. Biotechnol. 38(2):24-39. 100 % soybean flour. Header units g/mL look wrong for OAC/WAC (values 108 and 81), assumed %.
- odedeji2024: Odedeji et al. 2024, J. Underutilized Legumes 6(1):43-53. Unsprouted jack bean only; the sprouting-day labels in that paper are internally inconsistent so its germination rows were not used.
- abioye2014 (0 h row): see below.
- Unit handling: WAC and OAC printed as % are divided by 100 to g/g and flagged `calculated`. This assumes % means g absorbed per 100 g flour. Other properties keep the printed unit.
- Excluded: Okorie 2018 (white melon, Granthaalayah): bulk density 1.62 g/mL and foam capacity 0.03 % look implausible. Eshun 2022 thesis tables are literature ranges, not measurements. Audu & Aremu 2015 (black turtle bean) is not a target crop.

### Treatment experiments (data/treatment_experiments.csv), 58 rows
- abioye2014: Abioye, Adegbola & Onidare 2014, LAUTECH J. Eng. Technol. 8(2):176-181. African yam bean, soaked 9 h, germinated at 25 C for 24/48/72/96 h; baseline is the 0 h sample. 36 rows. Drying method not stated; the text and tables differ slightly on protein.
- chude2018: Chude, Amadi & Okoyeuzu 2018, Adv. Life Sci. Technol. 64:35-41. Bambara, 3-day lactic fermentation, 10 rows. Seeds were cooked before fermenting. Minor internal inconsistencies in the source.
- abulude2006: boiled / toasted / germinated vs raw melon seed, 12 rows. Times and temperatures not available.

### Still missing

- sides.csv, dishes.json, applications.json, playbook.json, safety_rules.json still empty.

## Added Nov 20 (batch 2)
- makinde2020: Makinde & Abolarin 2020, J. Appl. Sci. Environ. Manage. 24(9):1641-1647, open access. Dehulled cowpea, 3 genotypes (IT99K-573-2-1, IT96D-610, IT07K-292-10). Baseline = raw dehulled seed (soaked 5 min, then dehulled). Treatments: boiling (100 C, 20 min), pan roasting (about 20 min), autoclaving (121 C, 10 min); then oven-dried at 60 C and milled.
  - 81 treatment rows (E059-E139): tannin, phytate, oxalate, water and oil absorption, swelling power, starch solubility, loose and packed bulk density.
  - 6 functional rows: raw dehulled cowpea flour, water absorption (printed mL/g, taken as g/g, so flagged `calculated`) and loose bulk density.
  - Caveat: the paper's text calls its figures "reduction", but they are the percent remaining (e.g. tannin 40.37 / 121.80 = 33.1 % remaining, which the text calls "33.2 % reduction"). Change was computed from the table values, not the text.
  - Caveat: roasting temperature is not stated beyond the pan start of 60-70 C, so `temp_c` is blank for roasting.
  - Swelling power (Makinde) and swelling capacity (Abioye) are different methods and are kept as separate metrics.
  - Transcribed by text extraction; not yet checked against the PDF.
- Metric names were unified across papers (e.g. wac_pct -> water_absorption, phytate_mg100g -> phytate); the map is in training/mappings/metric_renames.csv. Abulude's "toasting" is now "roasting"; the original word is kept in method_note.

## Added Nov 20 (batch 3, training/data/new_rows/batch3/)
All values were read from PDF tables by text extraction or from the page image and are NOT yet checked against the PDFs. Treatment rows are E140-E305 (source ids B3-001 to B3-166 in method_note). `page` may hold a table label where the journal page was not read.
- abiodun2011: Abiodun & Adeleke 2011. Cowpea, pigeon pea, African yam bean.
  - 30 treatment rows: `dehulling_soaking` (dehull plus 30 min soak) vs raw whole-seed flour (baseline_type raw).
  - 6 functional rows: raw bulk density and water absorption.
  - Antinutrients are printed in mg/kg (Makinde uses g/kg, Sobowale mg/100 g); percent change is unit-free.
  - Cowpea water absorption of 0.9 mL/g (taken as g/g) is far below Makinde's 2.33 g/g and is probably a different method; QC flags it (`this_value_outlier`).
  - Trypsin inhibitor is printed with one decimal (1.0 to 0.5), so its percent change is coarse.
  - `swelling_index` is a different method from `swelling_power` and `swelling_capacity`.
- sobowale2025: Sobowale et al. 2025. Pigeon pea fermented 24/48/72 h after germination, by part (whole grain, dehulled grain, rootless, bran).
  - 106 treatment rows; baseline = germinated, unfermented flour (baseline_type germinated, not raw).
  - Table 5 (tannin, phytate, oxalate, all mg/100 g) has these problems:
    - Bran tannin at 48 h and 72 h is printed as 8.68 ± 0.63, the same as rootless 48 h, so it looks copied; those two rows were left out.
    - Phytate of about 1 mg/100 g is far below other papers; the unit may be mg/g or g/100 g.
    - Tannin jumps between hours (2.58, 10.34, 4.61, 9.23 for whole grain).
    - All Sobowale tannin and phytate rows are marked NEEDS_HUMAN in method_note and excluded from model training (training/mappings/training_exclusions.csv).
  - The table's separate whole-grain series (UWGF, WGFF24 to WGFF72) was not added.
- mubaiwa2018: Mubaiwa et al. 2018. Bambara groundnut, red and black-eye varieties.
  - 30 treatment rows: soaking, dry roasting (`roasting`), soaking + roasting, vs raw (baseline_type raw).
  - 10 raw functional rows.
  - Least gelation integers were read by OCR; check them.
  - Soaking time and roasting temperature were not captured.
  - Water and oil absorption were not read and are not included.
- adeleke2018: Adeleke et al. 2018. 18 whole-flour functional rows for 3 bambara cultivars x 6 properties. WAC and OAC were printed in % and divided by 100 to g/g (`calculated`); WAC 108-139 % means 1.1-1.4 g/g.
- ogunyinka2017: Ogunyinka et al. 2017. 4 functional rows for African locust bean flour made from FERMENTED seed, not raw (QC flag `processed_flour`). WAC and OAC were left out because the table gives no units.
- Batch 2 note: the original batch-2 files in training/data/new_rows/ were overwritten by the batch-3 files. Batch 2 was rebuilt from data/ into new_rows/batch2/ (see RECONSTRUCTED.txt); values are unchanged.

## Added Nov 20 (batch 4, training/data/new_rows/batch4/)
Treatment rows E306-E604 (source ids B4-... in method_note), plus 16 functional rows. All were transcribed from PDF tables (text extraction or page image) and are NOT yet checked against the PDFs.
data/treatment_experiments.csv now carries derived columns from training/mappings/: baseline_type, treatment_family, crop_group, in_ingredient_table, cumulative_from_raw, stage_order, route, stage_path.
- bolade2015: cowpea, 4 varieties (IT93K-452-1, IT95K-499s-35, IT97K-568-18, Ewa-oloyin).
  - 84 rows: household akara, moin-moin and gbegiri steps; trypsin inhibitor, phytate, tannin.
  - Stages are CUMULATIVE from raw seed (cumulative_from_raw = true). For example, deep_frying = soak > dehull > soak > wet mill > deep fry, not frying alone. Stage order and path are in the table. For pressure cooking (gbegiri route), the exact preceding stage is not stated.
  - Values are means of three households. Tannin magnitudes are large (about 2000+, mg/100 g as printed); percent change is unit-free.
- akubor2017: pigeon pea; roasting (paper word: toasting, 120 C 30 min), boiling, germination, fermentation; tannin, trypsin inhibitor; baseline hydrated + dehulled flour; 34 rows + 7 functional rows.
  - Left out: boiled tannin printed "25 +0.4" (probably 2.5), and toasted foam stability "2. 0e".
  - Germinated trypsin inhibitor "0.08 +0.7" has an SD larger than the mean.
- sharma2019: pigeon pea (variety AL 201, Punjab, India); germination at 25/30/35 C for 12-48 h plus a soak-only control; baseline non-germinated flour; 91 rows + 7 functional rows.
  - Emulsion activity is kept separate from emulsion capacity. The text's emulsion activity range does not match the table.
  - Foam stability was left out (multi-time-point definition).
- kewuyemi2024: whole cowpea, germination and fermentation (35 C, 48 h); tannin, oxalate; baseline raw whole flour; 4 rows.
- pokharel2024: mungbean (Nepal), a DIFFERENT CROP (evidence only, not an ingredient); 8 treatments; tannin, oxalate, phytate, saponin; baseline raw seed (dry basis); 32 rows. Soaking time: the methods say 18 h, the abstract and other sections 12 h; 12 h was used.
- ojha2020: horse gram (Nepal), a DIFFERENT CROP (evidence only); soaking, roasting, germination, fermentation; bulk density, water and oil absorption; 12 rows.
  - Water and oil absorption units are not printed (assumed g/g).
  - Antinutrients appear only in a bar chart (text gives percent reductions only) and were not added.
- gandhi2022: lentil (India), a DIFFERENT CROP (evidence only); soaking, boiling, germination; functional metrics; 34 rows.
  - Emulsion was not determined for boiled samples (cells left out).
  - Tannin and phytate appear only in a figure and were not added.
- kanetro2021: jack bean (Indonesia); soaking, germination 24/48/72 h; oil and water absorption; 8 rows + 2 functional rows.
  - Printed as % but the method gives g/g.
  - Control flour moisture is 13.3 % vs 7 % for treated flour.
  - Emulsion and foaming values were left out (units unclear).
- Read but not used: Jaichand 2020 (antinutrients only in figures; baseline soaked), Mashau 2025 (antinutrients only in figures), Adediran 2013 (only jack bean and cowpea blends), the grey pea 2024 paper (WAI/WSI, different measures).
- Processing models now ship for tannin, phytate and trypsin inhibitor (C3, shrunk mean per treatment), oxalate (ridge) and bulk density (C5, linear, in range only); see reports/candidates.md. C3 values are pooled across crops (including mungbean, lentil or horse gram where listed); every entry in bundle/model.json names its crops and citations.
